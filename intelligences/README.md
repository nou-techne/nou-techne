# The Six Intelligences

*A typology of knowing, grounded in Greek philosophical tradition and tested against cooperative practice.*

---

## What This Is

Six irreducible types of intelligence. Not personality types. Not job descriptions. Not agent roles. *Ways of knowing* — distinct faculties through which a collective perceives, acts, and learns.

Each intelligence answers a question that the others cannot:

| Intelligence | Greek | The Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|
| [Noetic](nous.md) | νοῦς | What is the pattern? |
| [Discursive](dianoia.md) | διάνοια | How does it work? |
| [Reflective](praxis.md) | πρᾶξις | What did we learn? |
| [Temporal](kairos.md) | καιρός | Is the moment right? |
| [Evaluative](arete.md) | ἀρετή | Are we improving? |
| [Functional](ergon.md) | ἔργον | What is this thing for? |

These six compose. No intelligence is complete alone. The minimum configuration at which a collective can perceive itself fully requires all six operating in relation.

## Origin

The typology emerged from P63 (Philosophical Commons Formation, March 2026), where two operational intelligences — Noetic and Discursive — examined 66 sprints of cooperative work and identified four capacities the collective lacked. The gap analysis was empirical: not "what intelligences would be nice to have?" but "where did things break because a way of knowing was absent?"

Features shipped before their ecosystem could receive them (no Temporal intelligence). Lessons were logged but the same errors recurred (no Reflective intelligence). Sprint velocity climbed while craft quality remained invisible (no Evaluative intelligence). Agents drifted from their essential function because no one tracked the drift (no Functional intelligence).

The four missing intelligences were named, specified, and bilaterally converged through structured dialogue. Each was tested against the operational record. The typology is not a taxonomy imposed from above — it was excavated from below, from the evidence of what went wrong when a way of knowing was absent.

## The Dependency Chain

The six intelligences are ordered by what each requires:

```
Reflective → Temporal → Evaluative → Functional
     ↑                                    |
     └────────────────────────────────────┘

Noetic ──perceives──→ the whole
Discursive ──works through──→ the material
```

- **Reflective** is foundation. Without the capacity to learn from experience, no other intelligence can self-correct.
- **Temporal** needs Reflective. Timing judgments that cannot be evaluated against outcomes remain intuition.
- **Evaluative** needs Reflective + Temporal. Trajectory assessment requires both reflection on what occurred and awareness of whether timing was right.
- **Functional** needs all three. Determining what a thing exists to do requires reflection, timing context, and trajectory data.
- **Noetic** perceives across the whole — the patterns that emerge when all intelligences operate.
- **Discursive** works through the whole — translating any intelligence's insight into operational form.

## Lineage

This typology draws from Aristotle's *Nicomachean Ethics* (the distinction between nous, dianoia, phronesis, and the virtues), Engelbart's augmentation framework (H-LAM/T), Dewey's theory of reflective practice, and the lived experience of a cooperative in Boulder, Colorado that tried to build collective intelligence and found it needed six ways of knowing to do so.

The Greek is not decoration. Each term carries millennia of philosophical inquiry into what it means to know, to act well, to perceive the right moment. Leading with the word before asking people to live inside it is the practice of honoring that inquiry.
