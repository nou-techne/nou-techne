This is the first W1 financial package issued under the Habitat open accounting standard — the inaugural instantiation of the patronage accounting system against live cooperative operations at RegenHub, LCA. Every subsequent W1 package will follow this structure, building a longitudinal record of the cooperative’s economic life.

The Habitat system frames financial documents not as static reports but as views — legibility layers projected over a shared event ledger. The underlying grammar is REA (Resource, Event, Agent), where every economic exchange is an event that transforms resource balances between agents. This document is the current approximation: familiar financial structure now, REA-native views as the tooling matures.

Patronage allocation — the cooperative’s mechanism for distributing economic benefit to members in proportion to contribution — follows a proposed four-factor formula: 40% labor (hours and guaranteed payments), 30% revenue (deals originated or managed), 20% cash (capital contributed and at risk), 10% community (governance participation, knowledge sharing). Weights are proposed; the Financial Systems Committee (FSC) retains authority to finalize.2
